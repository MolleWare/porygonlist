PorygonList keeps shopping lists, to-dos and any other quick list in step between the people who share them — straight from one phone to the other, with no account to make and no server in between.

<b>How sharing works</b>
Pair two phones once by scanning a code. After that, a shared list hands its changes over whenever both phones are on a wifi you have approved, such as your home network. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing ever goes to the internet. Away from a shared wifi, a list can travel as one line of text that the other person pastes back in.

<b>What it does</b>
• Several lists, each shared with whoever you choose, or kept to yourself
• Add, tick off, rename and remove items; changes from both sides merge
• If the same item is changed on two phones at once, you are asked which to keep
• Put items in order — the order is shared, so sorting by aisle works for everyone
• Arrange and pin your lists, just for you, and merge two lists into one
• Staples for the things you buy every week, learned from the lists you clear
• Recipes: pick what you need from a dish's ingredients and add it in one go, marked with the dish's name
• A list's history: put back what was cleared, or save it as a recipe
• A shopping view for the shop itself, where ticked items sink to the bottom
• Choose an avatar; it and your name reach everyone you share a list with
• Background sync, one switch: with the app closed, it syncs for half a minute on every quarter hour, only while you are on an approved wifi

<b>Good to know</b>
• Lists are meant to be thrown away. They live only on the phones that share them: uninstall the app and that phone's lists go with it.
• Two phones only swap changes while both can be reached on the same wifi. With background sync on, a closed phone wakes at :00, :15, :30 and :45, so it catches up within about a quarter of an hour, and two closed phones meet then. It needs alarms allowed; with it off, a phone syncs only while the app is open.
• A VPN hides which wifi you are on, so nothing syncs while one is on — unless your VPN app leaves PorygonList out of it (split tunnelling, excluding PorygonList).

<b>Permissions</b>
• Network access, to talk to paired phones on your local wifi
• Location (optional), only because Android hides the wifi's name without it — the app uses it to show that name and works fine if you say no
• Run at startup, so background sync carries on after a restart
• Alarms (optional), for background sync, which wakes on the exact quarter hour so closed phones sync at the same moment; without them it stays off
• Foreground service, to stay on the wifi for that half minute; Android may show a quiet notification while it runs
